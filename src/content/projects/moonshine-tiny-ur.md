I fine-tuned **Moonshine Tiny** for Urdu speech recognition and released the **27.1M-parameter** model with training sources and evaluation results.

On the first 100 eligible UrduSpeech test clips, the model card reports **24.3% WER / 11.7% CER**. These are self-reported results on that subset. The card also reports results for FLEURS and Common Voice Urdu.

[Explore the model and evaluation](https://huggingface.co/mahwizzzz/moonshine-tiny-ur).
