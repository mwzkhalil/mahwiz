I distilled a female Urdu text-to-speech voice into a 61 MB ONNX model that runs on CPUs. The training pipeline used more than 32,000 filtered synthetic speech samples.

The voice is available as a Piper model. [Read the technical write-up](https://huggingface.co/blog/mahwizzzz/distilling-omnivoice-into-aegis) or [get the model](https://huggingface.co/mahwizzzz/piper-voice-ur-aegis-female).
