**[Try the three Urdu voices in Cadenza TTS](https://huggingface.co/spaces/mahwizzzz/cadenza-tts).**

One of the voices is Aegis, a **61 MB ONNX** female Urdu voice for CPU inference. I created it by synthesizing speech with OmniVoice, filtering **33,288** clips, and training a Piper VITS student.
