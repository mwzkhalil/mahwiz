Lafzyn converts Urdu script to IPA phonetic transcription. I fine-tuned a Qwen3.5-0.8B model for the task and released a [live demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/mahwizzzz/lafzyn) and [model card](https://huggingface.co/mahwizzzz/lafzyn).
